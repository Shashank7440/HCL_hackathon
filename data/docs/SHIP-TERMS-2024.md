# ShopEasy Shipping and Delivery Terms (version 1.0)

## 1 Delivery timelines
Orders are typically delivered within 2-6 business days. Delays during peak periods are announced on the website.

## 2 Fees
### 2.1 Forward shipping
Forward shipping is free above INR 999 per order; below that a flat INR 49 applies.
### 2.2 International delivery
We do not currently ship outside India.
### 2.3 Reverse shipping fee
Returns are collected by ShopEasy's courier partners. A reverse shipping fee of INR 60 per return applies and is deducted from the refund. The fee is waived when the return reason is DEFECTIVE or DAMAGED.

## 3 Undelivered orders
If an order is returned to us undelivered, we will contact you to arrange re-delivery or a refund.
