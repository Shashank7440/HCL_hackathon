# ShopEasy Return, Refund and Exchange Policy (version 3.1)

## 1 Purpose
This policy governs returns, refunds and exchanges for all products sold by ShopEasy, effective from 2024-07-01. It is issued by the Legal and Customer Experience office.

## 2 Category eligibility
### 2.1 Eligible categories
Most products may be returned within the applicable return window (section 3), subject to the fees in section 4.
### 2.2 Condition requirements
Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all accessories and the invoice. Electronics must include all cables and manuals.
### 2.3 Hygiene items
Sealed personal-care products cannot be returned once opened.
### 2.4 Non-returnable categories
Grocery items (including beverages, food and consumable supplements) are not returnable under any circumstances. This does not affect your statutory rights.

## 3 Return windows
### 3.1 Windows by category
The return window is counted in calendar days from the delivery date and the item is eligible on the last day of the window:
- ELECTRONICS: 10 days
- FASHION: 30 days
- HOME: 15 days
- BEAUTY: 7 days
- GROCERY: non-returnable (section 2.4)
### 3.2 Exceptions
Extensions may be granted during service disruptions at ShopEasy's discretion, announced by circular.

## 4 Fees and limits
### 4.1 Restocking fee
For returns where the reason is CHANGED_MIND, a restocking fee of 10% of the net amount paid applies. The fee does not apply for DEFECTIVE, DAMAGED or WRONG_ITEM returns.
### 4.2 Tier waiver
The restocking fee is waived (0%) for GOLD and PLATINUM tier customers.
### 4.3 Approval limit
Refunds above INR 50,000 require human approval before processing and may take additional time.
### 4.4 Reverse shipping
The reverse shipping fee is defined in the Shipping and Delivery Terms (SHIP-TERMS-2024, section 2.3).

## 5 How to raise a return
Contact support with your order id; the agent will verify eligibility, quote the refund and raise the return. A return may be raised once per item.

## 6 Refund basis
Refunds are computed on the net price actually paid (after coupons and discounts), not the list price, minus applicable fees. Totals are never estimated.
