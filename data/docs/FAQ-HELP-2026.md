# ShopEasy Help Centre FAQ (version 5.0)

## Q1 How do I track my order?
Open your account page, choose Orders, and select the order. Statuses move from PLACED to SHIPPED to DELIVERED.

## Q2 How do refunds work?
Refunds are credited to your original payment method after the returned item is picked up and inspected.

## Q3 How long do I have to return something?
Most items can be returned within 15 days of delivery. Electronics may have shorter windows; check the product page or ask support.

## Q4 Can I exchange for a different size?
Yes, for FASHION items within the return window, subject to stock availability.

## Q5 Do you deliver internationally?
Please contact support for delivery availability in your area.
