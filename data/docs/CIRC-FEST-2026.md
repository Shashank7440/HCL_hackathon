# Festive Season Circular: Fashion Returns (CIRC-FEST-2026, version 1.0)

## 1 Extended fashion return window for the festive season
Effective from 2026-11-01, customers may return FASHION products within 45 calendar days of delivery, in place of the standard 30-day window, for all orders delivered during the festive promotion period.

## 2 Scope
This applies to FASHION items only and to all customer tiers. Until the effective date, the standard Return, Refund and Exchange Policy window (30 days) continues to apply.
